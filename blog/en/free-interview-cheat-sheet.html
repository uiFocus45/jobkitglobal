The Interview Cheat Sheet

The 10 questions you will almost certainly be asked, how to answer them, and what to do before, during and after the interview

The STAR method in 30 seconds

  1. Situation set the scene in one sentence.
  2. Task what were you responsible for?
  3. Action what did YOU do? This is the longest part.
  4. Result what happened? Add a number if you can.

The 10 questions you will almost certainly hear

  • Tell me about yourself present, one key achievement, why this job is your next step (60 to 90 seconds).
  • Why do you want this job? Link the role, the company and your strengths.
  • What do you know about us? Products, customers, one recent piece of news.
  • What are your strengths? Two strengths from the job ad, each with a short proof.
  • What is your weakness? A real one, not essential to the job, plus what you do to improve.
  • Tell me about a challenge. Use STAR, focus on your actions.
  • Why are you leaving your job? Talk about what you are moving toward, never criticize.
  • Why should we hire you? Match 3 requirements of the job with 3 proofs.
  • What are your salary expectations? A researched range, not a single number.
  • Do you have questions for us? Always yes 2 or 3 prepared questions.

Before, during, after

Do

  • Re-read the job ad and your resume
  • Prepare 3 short STAR stories
  • Test your camera and mic for a video call
  • Arrive or log in 10 minutes early
  • Send a thank-you email within 24 hours

Don't

  • Improvise your answer to Tell me about yourself
  • Criticize a former employer
  • Invent experience or numbers
  • Say you have no questions
  • Forget to ask about next steps
Thank-you email to copy

Subject: Thank you for today's interview

Hello [Name],

Thank you for taking the time to meet with me today about the [Job title] role. I enjoyed learning about [specific topic you discussed], and I'm even more motivated to join your team.

Please let me know if you need any further information.

Best regards, [Your name]

One hour before the interview

  • I can introduce myself in 90 seconds
  • I have 3 STAR stories ready
  • I know 3 facts about the company
  • I have my salary range in mind
  • I have 2 or 3 questions to ask
  • I know the name of the person I'm meeting

You've got this. Prepare well, stay yourself, and good luck with your interview.
